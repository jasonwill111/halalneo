## ⚠️ Critical Reminders

1. **Never keyword stuff** - Write for humans first, search engines second
2. **Consistency is key** - Publish new content weekly
3. **Monitor performance** - Check rankingsweekly, adjust strategy monthly
4. **Quality over quantity** - 1 great article > 10 mediocre ones
5. **Mobile-first** - Ensure all content is mobile-optimized
