## Question

Research the most important halal markets, to feed the knowledge base's **Country / Market Guides** section.

- Pick ~6–8 priority markets (e.g. Indonesia, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Turkey, Pakistan, Singapore, UK) — for each: market size/outlook for halal food, recognised certifying bodies, import requirements, and notable B2B buying characteristics.
- Note which markets accept foreign certification vs require in-country endorsement (the mutual-recognition landscape).

Capture findings as `## Answer` below, with sources.

## Answer

Full findings in [research/05-country-market-guides.md](../research/05-country-market-guides.md). Gist: 8 priority markets (Indonesia, Malaysia, Saudi, UAE, Turkey, Pakistan, Singapore, UK). Recognition splits into three tiers: mandatory-authority-with-MRA-list (Indonesia, Malaysia, Saudi, UAE, Singapore), accreditation-gated (Turkey/HAK), voluntary/unregulated (UK, Pakistan). Indonesia's 17 Oct 2026 mandatory halal expansion is the single biggest market event for exporters. Across Muslim-majority markets, a recognised halal cert is a listing precondition, not a differentiator.
