# Halal logistics & supply chain
Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Research halal logistics and supply chain requirements, to feed the knowledge base's **Logistics & Supply Chain** section.

- Halal logistics certification and standards (e.g. MS2400, HAS 23000 logistics scope, GPAI) and what "halal logistics" actually means in practice.
- Cold chain considerations for perishable halal goods (meat, dairy, prepared foods).
- Freight modes (sea/air/road), shipping documents, and customs clearance for halal products.
- Segregation/contamination-avoidance practices during storage and transport.

Capture findings as `## Answer` below, with sources.

## Answer

Full findings in [research/03-logistics.md](../research/03-logistics.md). Gist: halal logistics is a certified process discipline (segregation, contamination avoidance, traceability, temperature control) layered on food-safety logistics — not a product label. Regulatory spine: MS 2400:2019 (Malaysia), HAS 23000-5 + mandatory logistics certification under PP 39/2021 (Indonesia), OIC/SMIIC 17:2020 with Gulf adoptions. Cold chain: chilled 0–5°C, frozen −18°C, documented monitoring + segregation. Destination-country customs recognition is the common rejection point. Note: "GPAI" maps to no halal-logistics standard — treat as HDC/Malaysia Global Programme, verify meaning.
