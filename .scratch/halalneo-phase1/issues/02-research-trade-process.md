# B2B trade & sourcing process
Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Research the end-to-end B2B trade process for halal goods, to feed the knowledge base's **Trade & Sourcing** section.

- The typical B2B sourcing journey: inquiry → quotation → negotiation → sample/approval → contract → payment (T/T, L/C, etc.) → production → inspection → shipment → customs clearance.
- Halal-specific considerations at each step (documentation, certs accompanying shipment, halal logistics handoff).
- Incoterms basics and what a buyer/seller should know.
- Payment methods commonly used in cross-border halal trade and their risk profiles.

Capture findings as `## Answer` below, with sources.

## Answer

Full findings in [research/02-trade-process.md](../research/02-trade-process.md). Gist: 10-stage sourcing journey (inquiry → proforma → negotiation → sample → contract → payment → production → inspection → shipment → customs). Halal compliance is a parallel documentation layer at every stage — the halal certificate is a commercial document, shipment/batch-specific, from a destination-recognised body, and must match invoice/BOL wording verbatim under an L/C. Covers Incoterms 2020 (EXW→DDP risk ladder) and payment risk spectrum (T/T, L/C, D/P, escrow, open account).
