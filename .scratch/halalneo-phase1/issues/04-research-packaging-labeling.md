# Packaging & labeling requirements
Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

Research packaging and labeling requirements for halal food products, to feed the knowledge base's **Packaging & Labeling** section.

- Mandatory label elements for food products (ingredients, allergens, nutrition, origin, batch/expiry).
- Halal logo / mark usage rules — which logos are regulated, by whom, and the difference between a halal logo and a claim.
- Halal packaging materials considerations (e.g. no animal-derived material conflicts, cross-contamination).
- Common pitfalls that cause rejected imports/label audits.

Capture findings as `## Answer` below, with sources.

## Answer

Full findings in [research/04-packaging-labeling.md](../research/04-packaging-labeling.md). Gist: two compliance layers — general food labeling law (Codex CXS 1-1985: ingredients, allergens, net contents, origin, lot, dates) and halal-specific rules (Codex CAC/GL 24-1997 + importing-market regs). Distinguish the unregulated "halal" claim from licensed certifying-body logos (JAKIM, IFANCA, ISNA); some jurisdictions legally require the certifier's full name on pack. Packaging must avoid haram/najis materials (gelatin, tallow, lard, alcohol-based inks) per SMIIC 1 / GSO 2652. Audit pitfalls: ingredient-declaration errors, Arabic translation failures, unsupported claims, logo/cert misuse.
